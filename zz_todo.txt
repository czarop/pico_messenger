Its core responsibility is the wake → acquire → publish → sleep cycle. Sketch:
On wake:

Send MqttCommand::Start → watch MQTT_STATE until MqttReady
Send GnssCommand::Start → watch GNSS_STATE until Fix
Both ready → build payload, send MqttPublish via COMMAND_CHANNEL, await PUBLISH_RESULT
Send MqttCommand::Stop, GnssCommand::Stop
Sleep (POWMAN)

Motion state machine running alongside:

IMU reports arrive via Signal/Channel from imu_task
Moving → short wake interval (e.g. 15 min)
Stationary → long interval or dormant sleep until significant motion

Edge cases to handle:

GNSS fix timeout — publish anyway with last known location or no location
MQTT stack fails to come up — retry or skip publish, go back to sleep
GNSS_STATE::Error / MQTT_STATE down — handle gracefully, don't hang

No URC subscription needed — everything arrives via GNSS_STATE watch, MQTT_STATE watch, PUBLISH_RESULT signal, and IMU channel.
